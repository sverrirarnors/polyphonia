## <span id="why">Why support us?</span>

UO Polyphonia Zürich is a fixture of musical life at Zurich's universities and brings together students from around the world. Help us bring future projects to life — by joining the **Circle of Friends** or with a concrete **Patronage**.

Donations are tax-deductible. If you have any questions, please reach us at [sponsoring@polyphonia.ch](mailto:sponsoring@polyphonia.ch).

Thank you very much for your support!
