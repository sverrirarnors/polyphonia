# Join Polyphonia

We are currently not recruiting new members for the autumn 2026 semester. We will advertise vacancies for the next semester here in January 2027.

Our vacancies are open to students and staff of ETH Zürich and the University of Zürich.

## How to Apply

Once vacancies are advertised, please send an email to [besetzung@polyphonia.ch](mailto:besetzung@polyphonia.ch) with:
- Your name and instrument
- Your university affiliation (ETH/UZH, program of study)
- Brief description of your musical background
- Your availability for the advertised semester

We look forward to hearing from you!

<h2 id="audition-process">Audition Process</h2>

### For Strings
We will send you an excerpt from a specific piece, along with a string quartet, to prepare in advance. Together with the section leaders of the orchestra, you'll play these — giving you a feel for playing together in the orchestra and a chance to get to know us.


### For Wind and Brass
We will send you an excerpt (approx. 2–3 minutes) from a piece in our current program in advance. In addition, we ask you to prepare a piece of your choice of similar length and perform it for us.


## Requirements

- Currently enrolled as a student or employed as staff at ETH Zürich or University of Zürich
- Proficiency on your instrument
- Commitment to attend [weekly rehearsals](/schedule) (Wednesdays, 18:00-21:00)
- Enthusiasm for orchestral music and collaborative music-making
